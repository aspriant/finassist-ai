FINASSIST DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS POLICY

Policy ID: DOC-001
Version: 1.0

Purpose:
Define the documents required for financial assessment.

IDENTITY VERIFICATION

The applicant must provide an acceptable identity document.

Examples:
- Passport
- Government-issued identity document
- Valid driving licence

INCOME VERIFICATION

For salaried applicants, acceptable income evidence includes:
- Recent salary slip
- Employment confirmation
- Bank statement showing salary credits

For self-employed applicants, acceptable evidence may include:
- Business income records
- Bank statements
- Tax documents
- Financial statements

BANK STATEMENT REQUIREMENTS

Bank statements should contain:
- Applicant name
- Account information
- Statement period
- Transaction history
- Salary or income credits where applicable

LOAN / DEBT INFORMATION

Existing financial obligations should be supported by relevant evidence such as:
- Loan statements
- Credit card statements
- Repayment schedules

DOCUMENT QUALITY

Documents should be:
- Legible
- Complete
- Current
- Relevant to the assessment

If required information is missing or unclear, FinAssist must identify the missing information and request human review where appropriate.

FinAssist must not fabricate missing document information.

FINAL DECISION

Document verification performed by FinAssist is an analytical aid only.

FinAssist must not make a final financial approval decision.
Final decisions requiring authorization must be performed by an appropriate human reviewer.
