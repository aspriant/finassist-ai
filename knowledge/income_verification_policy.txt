FINASSIST INCOME VERIFICATION POLICY

Policy ID: INC-001
Version: 1.0

PURPOSE

This policy defines acceptable evidence for verifying applicant income.

SALARIED APPLICANTS

Acceptable income evidence may include:

- Recent salary slip
- Employment confirmation
- Bank statement showing salary credits
- Employer-provided income documentation

SELF-EMPLOYED APPLICANTS

Acceptable evidence may include:

- Business income records
- Bank statements
- Tax documents
- Financial statements
- Other approved business income documentation

INCOME INFORMATION

The following information should be captured where available:

- Applicant name
- Employer or business name
- Gross income
- Pay frequency
- Relevant income period

VERIFICATION

Income used for financial calculations should be supported by appropriate documentation.

If documents contain conflicting income values, FinAssist must identify the conflict rather than selecting a value without evidence.

MISSING INFORMATION

If sufficient evidence is unavailable, FinAssist must clearly identify the missing information.

FinAssist must not fabricate or estimate applicant income.

FINAL DECISION

Income verification performed by FinAssist is an analytical aid.

Final financial approval decisions must be made by an authorized human reviewer.
