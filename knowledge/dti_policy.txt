FINASSIST DTI POLICY

Policy ID: DTI-001
Version: 1.0

PURPOSE

This policy defines how Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI) should be calculated and reviewed.

DTI CALCULATION

DTI = (Total Monthly Debt Obligations / Gross Monthly Income) × 100

DTI BELOW 36%

A DTI below 36% is within the preferred range for this policy.

DTI FROM 36% TO 43%

A DTI from 36% through 43% requires additional review of the applicant's financial information.

DTI ABOVE 43%

A DTI above 43% requires manual risk review.

INCOME REQUIREMENT

The calculation must use verified gross monthly income.

DEBT REQUIREMENT

The calculation must include verified recurring monthly debt obligations.

DETERMINISTIC CALCULATION

Financial calculations should be performed using a deterministic financial calculation tool rather than relying on language-model arithmetic.

MISSING INFORMATION

If income or debt information is missing, FinAssist must not invent values. The missing information must be clearly identified.

FINAL DECISION

FinAssist must not make a final lending approval decision.

Cases requiring authorization or approval must be escalated to an appropriate human reviewer.
